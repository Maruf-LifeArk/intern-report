## Key Projects and Tasks

### 1. Industrial Energy & Machinery Monitoring System

**Project Description:**
A digital monitoring solution intended to provide centralized visibility into industrial energy usage and machinery-related operational data. The system was designed to support easier monitoring, reporting, and interpretation of industrial equipment and energy-related information.

**My Role and Responsibilities:**
As a Digital Solutions Intern, I primarily contributed to the **business analysis, system analysis, requirements documentation, UI/UX design, and prototype development** stages of the project.

My responsibilities included:

* Gathered and analyzed business and user requirements through discussions with the supervisor and relevant team members.
* Prepared and refined the **Software Requirements Specification (SRS)** and **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**.
* Documented system workflows and prepared supporting diagrams, including:

  * Data Flow Diagrams (DFD)
  * Use Case Diagrams
  * Entity Relationship (ER) Diagrams
* Suggested suitable **SDLC approaches and development methodologies** based on the project's requirements and workflow.
* Presented the prepared documentation and system concepts to the supervisor for review.
* Identified and corrected mistakes or inconsistencies based on supervisor feedback.
* Conducted additional research when requirements or technical concepts were unfamiliar.
* Designed the UI/UX concepts and user flows using **Figma**.
* Used **Claude Code** to develop interactive frontend prototypes from the approved design and workflow concepts.
* Used **Antigravity IDE** and Claude Code as development and analysis assistants for exploring implementation approaches, reviewing workflows, refining designs, and identifying potential issues rather than relying on AI output without validation.
* Collaborated with developers to communicate:

  * Database structure requirements
  * Frontend requirements
  * Backend requirements
  * System constraints and functional dependencies
* Used **Jira** and **Slack** for task coordination, communication, and project-related collaboration.

**Tools / Technologies Used:**
Figma, Claude Code, Antigravity IDE, Jira, Slack, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SRS/PRD documentation, DFD, UML Use Case Diagrams, ER Diagrams.

**Outcome / Result / Impact:**
The project resulted in a clearer and more structured representation of the proposed digital solution before implementation. The requirements documents, system diagrams, UI/UX designs, and interactive prototypes helped communicate the intended functionality between business and technical stakeholders and provided developers with clearer implementation requirements.
