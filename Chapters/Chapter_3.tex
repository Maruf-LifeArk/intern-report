\chapter{Specific Details on Training Activities and Report}
\label{chapter-3}

\section{Introduction}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The industrial training was carried out at Active Green Energy Ltd. from 23 July 2026 to 23 September 2026 (approximately 130 hours), in the Digital Solutions department.
    \item Key learning objectives included business analysis, requirements documentation, UI/UX design, and AI-assisted frontend prototyping for industrial digital products.
    \item Technologies and tools used included Figma, Jira, Slack, Claude Code, and Antigravity IDE, alongside SRS/PRD documentation and DFD, Use Case, and ER diagramming.
    \item Over the training period, this progressed from foundational requirement-gathering work to independently leading analysis and design work on two parallel projects, with growing autonomy in client-facing communication.
\end{itemize}

\section{Duties and Activities}

\subsection{Phase 1 (Weeks 1--3): Onboarding and Requirement Analysis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Orientation:} Introduction to the company, the Digital Solutions department, and internal workflow tools (Jira, Slack).
    \item \textbf{Key Activities Learned:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Business analysis fundamentals and the SDLC as applied within the company (see Figure~\ref{fig:sdlc}).
        \item Structure and purpose of Software Requirements Specification (SRS) and Product Requirements Document (PRD) files.
        \item Participation in initial client discussions, under supervision, to observe requirement-gathering in practice.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 2 (Weeks 4--6): Documentation and System Design}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hands-on Training:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Preparing and refining SRS and PRD documents based on gathered requirements.
        \item Designing Data Flow Diagrams (DFD), Use Case Diagrams, and Entity-Relationship (ER) Diagrams to represent proposed system workflows.
        \item Presenting draft documentation to the supervisor for review and revising based on feedback.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Tools Introduced:} Figma (UI/UX design), Jira (task tracking).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 3 (Weeks 7--9): UI/UX Design and Prototyping}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Design Work:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Translating documented requirements into user flows and interface concepts in Figma.
        \item Iterating on designs based on supervisor and, where applicable, client feedback.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{AI-Assisted Prototyping:} Using Claude Code and Antigravity IDE to convert approved Figma designs into interactive frontend prototypes (HTML/CSS/JavaScript), while manually reviewing and validating all AI-generated output against the original requirements before sharing it further.
    \item \textbf{Handoff:} Communicating database, frontend, backend, and system-constraint requirements to the development team ahead of implementation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Projects}

\subsection{Project 1: Industrial Energy \& Machinery Monitoring System}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Description:} A digital monitoring solution intended to provide centralized visibility into industrial energy usage and machinery-related operational data, supporting easier monitoring, reporting, and interpretation of equipment and energy information.
    \item \textbf{My Role and Contributions:} I contributed to the business analysis, system analysis, requirements documentation, UI/UX design, and prototype development stages. Specifically, I:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Gathered and analyzed business and user requirements through discussions with my supervisor and relevant team members.
        \item Prepared and refined the SRS and PRD for the system.
        \item Documented system workflows using DFDs, Use Case Diagrams, and ER Diagrams.
        \item Recommended suitable SDLC approaches and development methodologies based on the project's requirements.
        \item Presented the documentation and system concepts to my supervisor for review, and revised them based on feedback.
        \item Designed UI/UX concepts and user flows in Figma, then converted the approved designs into interactive frontend prototypes using Claude Code.
        \item Coordinated with developers on database structure, frontend/backend requirements, and system constraints.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Tools/Technologies Used:} Figma, Claude Code, Antigravity IDE, Jira, Slack, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SRS/PRD documentation, DFD, Use Case Diagrams, ER Diagrams.
    \item \textbf{Outcome/Impact:} The project produced a clearer, structured representation of the proposed digital solution ahead of implementation. The requirements documents, system diagrams, UI/UX designs, and interactive prototypes helped communicate the intended functionality between business and technical stakeholders and gave developers clearer implementation requirements.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Project 2: IIoT-Based Production Monitoring System}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Description:} A proposed Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) solution focused on collecting and presenting production-related industrial data in a centralized digital interface, intended to improve visibility into production activities.
    \item \textbf{My Role and Contributions:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Participated in the analysis of business and production-monitoring requirements.
        \item Helped translate operational requirements into functional system features and prepared corresponding sections of the SRS and PRD.
        \item Developed supporting workflow diagrams, including DFDs, Use Case Diagrams, and ER Diagrams.
        \item Reviewed the proposed architecture and data flow with my supervisor and incorporated feedback.
        \item Researched unfamiliar IIoT-related concepts required to understand the system workflow.
        \item Designed user flows, dashboards, and interface concepts in Figma, and converted selected concepts into interactive frontend prototypes using Claude Code.
        \item Coordinated with developers on expected data structures, frontend functionality, and system limitations.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Tools/Technologies Used:} Figma, Claude Code, Antigravity IDE, Jira, Slack, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SRS/PRD documentation, DFD, Use Case Diagrams, ER Diagrams.
    \item \textbf{Outcome/Impact:} The work produced structured requirements, system workflows, and interactive UI concepts for the proposed IIoT solution, providing a clearer foundation for future development and bridging industrial requirements with software implementation. This project also required managing a real scheduling risk -- a delayed factory visit by the IoT engineering team -- which is discussed further in Section~\ref{sec:risk-chapter1} of Chapter 1.
\end{itemize}
