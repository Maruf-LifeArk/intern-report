\section{Introduction}
\par Active Green Energy Ltd. is a Dhaka-based firm committed to providing holistic industrial energy and digital technology solutions. With strong commitments to reliability, precision, and customer success, the organization ensures its products, processes, and systems deliver exceptional quality. Combining engineering expertise with growing software and IoT capability, Active Green Energy Ltd. helps energy-intensive industrial clients improve operational efficiency, mitigate risk, and modernize their operations.

\section{Company Profile}
\par \textbf{Active Green Energy Ltd.} is an emerging technology and industrial energy solutions provider based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Incorporated in \textbf{2016}, the company specializes in supplying, managing, and optimizing electrical power and thermal systems for energy-intensive industries, and has since expanded into IoT-based industrial solutions and custom software development. The company employs approximately \textbf{50 people} across its engineering, business, and digital solutions functions.

\subsection{Historical Timeline}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{2016:} Incorporated as an industrial energy solutions provider in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
    \item \textbf{Growth phase:} Expanded its client base to include several large industrial conglomerates, supplying and optimizing electrical power and thermal systems.
    \item \textbf{Recent development:} Established a dedicated \textbf{Digital Solutions} department to extend the company's offering into IoT-enabled monitoring systems and custom software development for industrial clients.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Work Environment}
\par The Digital Solutions department follows a structured yet collaborative work environment, coordinating tasks through \textbf{Jira} and \textbf{Slack}, with regular feedback sessions between interns, business analysts, designers, and developers. As an intern, I was given direct access to client-facing requirement discussions under supervision, which allowed close mentorship while still contributing meaningfully to live projects.

\subsection{Mission and Vision}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mission:} To help energy-intensive industries improve efficiency, reliability, and operational visibility through integrated energy management and digital solutions.
    \item \textbf{Vision:} To grow as a technology-enabled industrial solutions provider, combining traditional energy and machinery expertise with modern IoT and software capability.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Core Values}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Reliability and precision in engineering and energy solutions
    \item Client-centricity, particularly with long-term industrial clients
    \item Continuous adoption of modern technology, including IoT and AI-assisted digital tools
    \item Cross-functional teamwork between engineering, business, and technology teams
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Type of Organization}
\par Active Green Energy Ltd. operates as an established, hierarchically-structured industrial company, with the Digital Solutions department functioning more like an agile, project-based team within that larger structure. This hybrid arrangement allows the department to adopt Agile-style, iterative workflows -- rapid requirement gathering, prototyping, and feedback cycles -- while still reporting through the company's standard management hierarchy.

\subsection{Hierarchical Overview}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chairman \& Managing Director:} Provide overall strategic direction for the company.
    \item \textbf{Directors:} Oversee high-level coordination across business functions.
    \item \textbf{Department Heads:} Lead operational units such as Business \& Sales, Machinery Solutions, Projects \& Operations, and Digital Solutions.
    \item \textbf{Digital Solutions Team:} Business analysts, UI/UX designers, developers, and IoT engineers who design and build the company's digital products.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Roles and Responsibilities of Officials}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chairman:} Sets the company's overall strategic direction and represents the organization at the highest level.
    \item \textbf{Managing Director:} Oversees day-to-day operations across all departments and ensures alignment with company goals.
    \item \textbf{Directors:} Support the Managing Director in coordinating between departments and major client accounts.
    \item \textbf{Business Analyst (Digital Solutions):} Gathers and documents client requirements, bridges communication between clients and the technical team, and guides system analysis and design -- the role I worked most closely with during my internship.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Interactions with Various Departments}
\par During the internship, most day-to-day work was within the Digital Solutions department. However, the role also involved periodic interaction with the Business \& Sales department -- since client relationships and initial requirement leads typically originated there -- to ensure that proposed digital solutions aligned with client expectations set during the sales process.

\subsection{Collaboration with Team Leads}
\par My industrial supervisor, Md. Mochaddec Hossain (Business Analyst), served as the primary point of coordination for my work. Regular review sessions were used to validate requirement documents, review UI/UX designs, and check prototype progress before work was shared further with developers or clients.

\subsection{Cross-Functional Coordination}
\par The two projects I contributed to required coordination across business analysis, UI/UX design, and software development. Structured workflows -- including shared Jira boards, documented SRS/PRD files, and diagram-based specifications (DFD, Use Case, ER diagrams) -- enabled smooth handoff of requirements from the business analysis stage to the design and development stages.

\subsection{Interdepartmental Communication}
\par Communication was primarily maintained through Slack for day-to-day coordination and Jira for task and progress tracking, supplemented by direct review meetings with my supervisor and, for client-related matters, joint meetings involving both the client and relevant technical team members.

\section{Constructive Feedback About the Training Program}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Positive aspects:} I was given real client-facing responsibility early on rather than being limited to observation, and my supervisor consistently provided detailed feedback on my documentation and design work, which accelerated my learning.
    \item \textbf{Areas for improvement:} A more structured onboarding checklist for interns (covering internal documentation standards and tool access) at the start of the training period would have reduced the initial adjustment time.
    \item \textbf{Suggestions for future interns:} Future interns would benefit from being paired with both a business-side and a technical-side mentor from week one, since the Digital Solutions role sits directly between the two.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\par Active Green Energy Ltd. is an established industrial energy and machinery solutions provider that is actively expanding into IoT and digital solutions. With its hierarchical yet increasingly agile Digital Solutions division, and its focus on reliability and client-centricity, the company maintains strong industry standing while contributing to the digital transformation of Bangladesh's industrial sector. This combination of an established industrial foundation and a growing technology-driven division made it a valuable and instructive environment for professional development.
