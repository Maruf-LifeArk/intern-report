\par
This report summarizes the industrial training experience carried out at \textbf{Active Green Energy Ltd.}, a Dhaka-based technology and industrial energy solutions provider, in the role of \textbf{Digital Solutions Intern} within the Digital Solutions department. The primary objective of the training was to gain hands-on experience in business analysis and digital product design, and to enhance professional skills by contributing to real client-facing projects.

The training involved working with tools and techniques such as \textbf{Figma, Claude Code, Antigravity IDE, Jira, and Slack}, alongside requirements-engineering artifacts including \textbf{Software Requirements Specifications (SRS), Product Requirements Documents (PRD), Data Flow Diagrams (DFD), Use Case Diagrams, and Entity-Relationship (ER) Diagrams}, following industry-standard SDLC and Agile practices.

Tasks included gathering and analyzing business requirements directly from client meetings, documenting system workflows, designing user flows and interfaces, and building AI-assisted interactive frontend prototypes, allowing exploration of the practical aspects of business analysis, UI/UX design, and requirements-driven software development.

Throughout the training, involvement in two key projects -- an \textbf{Industrial Energy and Machinery Monitoring System} and an \textbf{IIoT-Based Production Monitoring System} -- helped strengthen problem-solving skills and technical competencies. Collaborative work with developers, designers, and testers, along with client-facing requirement-gathering sessions, contributed to improved communication and stakeholder-management abilities.

This internship provided a comprehensive learning experience, bridging academic knowledge with industry practice, and prepared me for future professional roles in the field of \textbf{business analysis and digital solution design}.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Business Analysis, Requirements Engineering, UI/UX Design, Industrial IoT, Digital Solutions, Prototyping
